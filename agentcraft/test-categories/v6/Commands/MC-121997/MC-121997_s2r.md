
BUG_ID: MC-121997

AFFECTED_VERSION: Minecraft Java Edition 1.17.1

BUG_BEHAVIOR:
The server maintains a separate functional world border for each dimension, while the Nether displays the Overworld border instead of its own functional border. Consequently, the visible border can disagree with the border reported and enforced in the Nether.

PRECONDITIONS:

- Run Minecraft Java Edition 1.17.1.
- Use a world with commands enabled.
- The player must have permission to use `/worldborder`, `/tp`, and `/gamemode`.
- PRACTICAL_ASSUMPTION: Use a new unmodified world with no bed or respawn anchor set. This prevents an existing border configuration or custom respawn location from affecting verification.
- Begin in the Overworld.

SETUP:

1. Enter Creative mode:

```mcfunction
/gamemode creative
```

2. Build a Nether portal:
   1. Open the Creative inventory.
   2. Obtain obsidian and flint and steel.
   3. Construct a vertical obsidian rectangle with an exterior size of 4 blocks wide and 5 blocks tall. Leave a 2-block-wide, 3-block-tall empty interior.
   4. Hold flint and steel in the main hand.
   5. Use it once on an obsidian block bordering the portal interior.
   6. Confirm that the interior fills with the purple portal texture.

3. While still in the Overworld, set the border diameter to 1000 blocks:

```mcfunction
/worldborder set 1000
```

VERIFY:

- Command feedback reports that the world border was set to 1000 blocks wide.
- Do not issue another world-border command in the Overworld.

REPRODUCTION_STEPS:

1. Enter the Nether:
   1. Walk into the purple portal interior.
   2. Remain inside until the dimension-loading screen appears.
   3. Wait until the Nether finishes loading.

VERIFY:

- The Nether environment is loaded.
- The player remains in Creative mode.

2. Teleport to the positive-Z edge of the 1000-block border:

```mcfunction
/tp @s 0 50 500
```

3. Without moving far from the teleport location, rotate the camera horizontally through 360 degrees and look for the translucent striped world-border wall.

VERIFY:

- A visible world-border wall appears at or near `Z=500`.
- This corresponds to the Overworld border diameter of 1000 blocks centered at `0,0`.

4. While still in the Nether, set the Nether’s border diameter to 10 blocks:

```mcfunction
/worldborder set 10
```

5. Keep observing the same area around `Z=500`. Rotate the camera horizontally again if necessary.

VERIFY:

- Buggy visual intermediate state: the visible border remains at or near the old 1000-block border position around `Z=500`.
- It does not visually relocate to approximately `Z=5` or `Z=-5`.

6. Query the functional border diameter while still in the Nether:

```mcfunction
/worldborder get
```

VERIFY:

- Command feedback reports a border diameter of 10 blocks.
- This conflicts with the visible border, which still appears to have a diameter of 1000 blocks.

7. PRACTICAL_ASSUMPTION: Teleport slightly inside the border that is visibly displayed, while remaining far outside the functional 10-block border:

```mcfunction
/tp @s 0 50 490
```

8. Switch to Survival mode:

```mcfunction
/gamemode survival
```

9. Do not cross the visible wall at `Z=500`. Wait for world-border damage.

VERIFY:

- The player takes damage and eventually dies despite being on the apparent inside of the visible 1000-block border.
- This demonstrates that the functional Nether border is actually 10 blocks wide and centered around `0,0`, even though that border is not displayed there.

10. Select Respawn on the death screen.

VERIFY:

- With the stated new-world prerequisite, the player respawns in the Overworld.

11. Return to Creative mode:

```mcfunction
/gamemode creative
```

12. Query the border diameter in the Overworld:

```mcfunction
/worldborder get
```

VERIFY:

- The Overworld reports a border diameter of 1000 blocks rather than 10 blocks.
- The Nether previously reported 10 blocks in the same world.

EXPECTED_BEHAVIOR:

- The world border should not maintain contradictory independent sizes across the Overworld and Nether.
- After setting the border to 10 blocks, its visible wall should match the functional border location, approximately 5 blocks from its center on each horizontal axis.
- Players located around `Z=490` should not see a misleading border wall farther away while receiving damage from an invisible border.

OBSERVED_BUG_BEHAVIOR:

- `/worldborder get` reports 10 blocks in the Nether but 1000 blocks in the Overworld.
- In the Nether, the rendered border remains at the Overworld’s approximately 1000-block-wide position.
- The functional 10-block Nether border remains enforced independently and damages a Survival player around `Z=490`, even though the displayed border suggests that location is inside the allowed area.

SUCCESS_CONDITION:

Reproduction succeeds if all of the following occur:

- `NETHER_BORDER_REPORTED_SIZE = 10`
- `OVERWORLD_BORDER_REPORTED_SIZE = 1000`
- `NETHER_VISIBLE_BORDER_POSITION ≈ Z=500`
- A Survival player at approximately `(0, 50, 490)` takes world-border damage without crossing the visible border.

FAILURE_OR_AMBIGUITY:

- The Nether terrain at `(0, 50, 500)` or `(0, 50, 490)` may obstruct the camera or cause unrelated suffocation, falling, fire, or lava damage. The report does not provide a seed or terrain preparation procedure. The command-result mismatch and unchanged visible border are still valid verification evidence even if the damage test is obstructed.
