## Description

The Bug:
Potions, tipped arrows, and dyed leather armor render with missing or incorrect colors in the inventory.
Steps to Reproduce:
- Switch to creative mode and enter “potion” into the search field.

- Observe the color of the potions you are shown.

Observed Behavior:
The said items are rendered with missing or incorrect colors in the inventory.
Expected Behavior:
The said items would be rendered with the correct colors in the inventory.
