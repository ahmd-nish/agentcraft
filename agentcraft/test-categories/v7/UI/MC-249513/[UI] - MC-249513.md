## Description

The bug
Frogspawn is not next to the turtle egg in the Creative inventory, despite both of them being mob egg blocks.
How to reproduce
- Be in Creative

- Look through the "Miscellaneous" tab

Expected results
The turtle egg and frogspawn would be next to each other.
Actual results
The turtle egg is one slot away from being as far away from frogspawn as physically and mathematically possible.
How to fix
Frogspawn should be moved to be directly after the turtle egg in the Miscellaneous tab.
Further notes
In addition to moving frogspawn next to the turtle egg, it may also be worth moving the chicken egg to just before it, as while it's not a block that hatches over time, it's still an egg, and would make more sense to be there.
In addition, the conduit should probably be moved to be directly next to the beacon as both are area-of-effect buff blocks; I figured it'd make sense to mention this here since the turtle egg is currently directly between both of them.
