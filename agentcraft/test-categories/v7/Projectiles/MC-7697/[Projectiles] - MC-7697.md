## Description

The Bug:
Tridents or arrows shot through lava sometimes don't catch fire.
Steps to Reproduce:
- Build the setup shown in the attachment below.

- Stand on the diamond block and throw/shoot multiple tridents or arrows through the lava.

- Take note as to whether or not tridents or arrows shot through lava sometimes don't catch fire.

Observed Behavior:
Tridents or arrows sometimes don't catch fire.
Expected Behavior:
Tridents or arrows would always catch fire.
