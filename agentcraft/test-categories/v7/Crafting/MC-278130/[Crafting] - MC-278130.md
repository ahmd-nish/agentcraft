## Description

The Bug:
Resin bricks cannot be placed or used inside smithing tables.
The 24w45a article states that:
Resin Brick is now used as the material for armor trimming instead of Resin Clump
Steps to Reproduce:
- Obtain any armor trim, any chestplate, and a resin brick.

- Place down a smithing table and attempt to apply the resin trim to the chestplate.

Observed Behavior:
Resin bricks cannot be placed or used inside smithing tables.
Expected Behavior:
Resin bricks would be able to be placed or used inside smithing tables.
