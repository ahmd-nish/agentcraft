## Description

The Acacia Hanging sign cannot be crafted due to an incorrect recipe causing a conflict.
Expected Outcome:
The Acacia Hanging sign should be crafted using 6 Stripped Acacia Logs & 2 Chains.
Actual Outcome:
The Acacia Hanging Sign cannot be crafted due to the recipe incorrectly requiring 6 Stripped Oak Logs not Acacia.
Steps to Reproduce:
1. Attempt to craft the Acacia Hanging Sign.
2. (optional) perform /recipe give @a *, and search Hanging Sign in the Crafting Table to see the recipe conflict.
